"Digitals India Showcases Cutting-Edge Safety Solutions at Fire India 2025"

Digitals India exhibited at Fire India 2025, held from 25th to 27th September at the Bombay Exhibition Center, NESCO, Mumbai.

The key highlight of the exhibition was the Fire Guard Series of conventional fire alarm panels, reflecting Digitals India’s commitment to innovation and reliability in life safety systems.

Another major attraction was the newly launched Smart Communicator, offering seamless integration across fire, security, CCTV, and access control systems. The event also featured Digitals India’s AI-assisted HMS solutions, designed to enhance real-time monitoring and intelligent safety management.

"Digitals India Showcased Cutting-Edge Security Solutions at Intersec 2025"

Dubai, UAE – January 16, 2025: Digitals India made a remarkable impact at Intersec 2025, held from January 14 to 16 at the Dubai World Trade Centre. The company exhibited its latest security alarm systems, fire alarm solutions, auto dialers, and independent alarm technologies, all proudly manufactured in India.

A key highlight was the visit of Mr. Satish Siwan Ji, Consul General of India, to Digitals India’s stall (8-B36), underscoring the significance of Indian innovation in the global security sector.

With a strong focus on international collaborations and expanding its global footprint, Digitals India engaged with channel partners and industry leaders, reinforcing its commitment to delivering world-class security solutions under the ‘Make in India’ initiative.

The event provided an excellent platform for showcasing advanced security technologies and fostering new business opportunities.

"Digitals India Revolutionizing Safety and Security with Innovation"
Digitals India Concludes Successful Participation at IFSEC India 2024

New Delhi, 12th-14th December 2024 – Digitals India, a leader in advanced technology solutions, showcased its innovative DI-HMS (Health Monitoring Solution) and Loop Fire Detection Solution at IFSEC India 2024, held at Pragati Maidan.

Attendees were impressed with the groundbreaking real-time health monitoring, advanced alert mechanisms, and data-driven reporting capabilities of DI-HMS. Additionally, the Loop Fire Detection Solution captured attention with its pinpoint notifications, sensor-driven technology, and enhanced safety features, redefining modern fire safety standards.

The event also highlighted IP-enabled Smart Fire Detection & Alarm Solutions, enabling centralized monitoring, quick integration, and proactive fire protection, along with Intelligent Security Solutions supporting industry-leading protocols like SIA DC-09 and Contact ID.

Digitals India extends gratitude to attendees, partners, and organizers for the overwhelming response, reaffirming its mission to deliver smarter, safer, and future-ready solutions. For more information, contact info@digitalsindia.com.
