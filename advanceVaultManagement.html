Advanced Vault Management System

Keys can be stolen. Digital access can't be picked.

The risk of theft is documented annually, and traditional key-based systems are failing to secure high-value assets. A single stolen key can lead to massive losses, as seen in a June 2026 incident where jewellery worth over ₹4 crore was removed in just 25 minutes due to the lack of an on-site alarm system.

7 Vault Challenges & Their Solutions

A closer look at the risks high-value vaults face today, and how a smarter, connected security approach closes every gap.

01

Stolen Key Vulnerability

Keyless Operation & Dual Authentication. A valid password is required; a stolen key alone cannot open the vault

02

After-Hours Breach Risk

Night-Time Locking. Access is permitted only within pre-approved hours, blocking off-hours entry.

03

Blind Spots in Compliance

Log Management. Every access attempt is logged with a timestamp for compliance and audits.

04

High Operational Downtime

Retrofit-Ready. Attaches to existing strongroom or locker doors — zero structural damage.

05

Forced Physical Entry

Break-In Proof Build. A thick, tamper-resistant steel casing built to defeat drills and forced entry.

06

Hostage & Duress Threats

Emergency Coercion Code. A special duress protocol silently alerts help during a hostage situation.

07

Fragmented Operations

24/7 Monitoring. All sites and devices are monitored centrally from a single dashboard.
