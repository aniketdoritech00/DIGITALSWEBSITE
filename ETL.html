Electronic Time Lock

Experience the next level of security with our Electronic Time Lock, designed to give you complete control from anywhere, anytime. Proudly Make in India and powered by cutting-edge technology, this advanced solution ensures unmatched reliability and convenience. Embrace the freedom to manage your locks remotely while securing your premises with precision-engineered innovation.

Key Features:
  • Programmed Operation: Opens only at programmed times with a password.
  • Emergency Override: Manual override available in case of system failure. Emergency switch locks the system for up to 4 hours during intrusion.
  • GSM Communication: Sends alarm events and logs to mobile devices and the Central Monitoring Station via SMS and alert calls.
  • Biometric & Printer Compatibility: Supports biometric authentication and can print log events using a thermal printer.
