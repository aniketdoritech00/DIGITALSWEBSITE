What is a GSM Autodialer?

A GSM autodialer is a critical device that connects with your alarm system and automatically sends voice calls and SMS alerts to predefined numbers when an event is triggered. Whether it's a security breach, fire incident, or system trigger—it ensures instant communication and faster emergency response without relying on internet connectivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GSM autodialer?

A GSM autodialer is a device that sends automatic voice call and SMS alerts to predefined numbers when an alarm event is triggered.

Does a GSM autodialer require internet?

No. It operates using a SIM card over the GSM network—fully functional even without Wi-Fi or broadband connectivity.

Where is a GSM autodialer used?

Banks, ATMs, industries, retail shops, homes, offices, and any location requiring instant remote alerting.
