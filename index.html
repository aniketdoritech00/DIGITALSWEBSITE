  1. Foundation Year

    1981

    The First Indian Electronic Token Display System for Banks is an innovative solution designed to streamline customer service in banking environments. This system integrates electronic displays to show token numbers, significantly improving the efficiency of customer flow management. It enhances the waiting experience by providing real-time updates, reducing confusion about queue status. The development of this system showcases a commitment to modernizing banking operations in India.

  2. India’s 1st Security System

    1983

    India's first indigenously designed security alarm system marks a pivotal advancement in the nation's defense and security technology. This innovative system is engineered to effectively deter intruders, providing enhanced safety for homes and businesses alike. By utilizing cutting-edge technology, it offers features such as real-time alerts and remote monitoring, ensuring users can respond swiftly to potential threats.

  10. First Emergency Auto Dialer

    1998

    We developed our first Emergency Auto Dialer, a crucial device engineered to enhance safety during emergencies. This reliable system automatically detects critical situations, such as accidents or health crises, and swiftly initiates calls to pre-configured contacts. By ensuring immediate communication, it empowers users to seek help promptly, potentially saving lives. Its user-friendly design and automated functionality make it accessible for various environments, from homes to workplaces.

  11. Micro Controller-Based Fire Alarm System

    2003

    We successfully launched a Microcontroller-Based Fire Alarm System that leverages advanced microcontroller technology for rapid fire detection. This system is designed to monitor environmental changes and detect smoke or heat, ensuring quick identification of fire hazards. Upon detection, it immediately activates alarms and sends alerts to designated contacts, facilitating prompt responses. With its reliable performance and efficiency, this fire alarm system enhances safety in residential and commercial spaces, providing peace of mind to users.

  17. India’s 1st Electronic Time Lock System

    2016

    India's first Electronic Time Lock System marks a significant advancement in securing currency chests in banking and financial institutions. This innovative technology utilizes sophisticated electronics to restrict access to authorized personnel during specific time periods. By integrating advanced locking mechanisms and digital monitoring, it enhances protection against unauthorized access and tampering. This system raises the standards of financial security in India, ensuring the integrity of banking operations.

  18. Standalone Shutter Guard

    2019

    The first stand-alone Electronic Shutter Guard System introduces an advanced solution for enhancing security through real-time monitoring. This innovative system is designed to detect any attempts to tamper with shutters or locks, triggering an immediate remote alarm. By utilizing cutting-edge sensors and alert mechanisms, it provides an effective deterrent against unauthorized access. This technology not only improves safety but also offers peace of mind for businesses and property owners, marking a significant advancement in security systems.

  19. India’s 1st IP Based Security Alarm System

    2021

    The first IP-based Security Alarm System revolutionizes security management by allowing remote monitoring and control over security features. This innovative system leverages Internet Protocol (IP) technology to provide real-time alerts and status updates, ensuring that users can respond to threats instantly, regardless of their location. The system also offers comprehensive reporting capabilities, allowing users to review security events and maintain detailed logs for analysis. This advancement not only enhances overall safety but also empowers users with greater control and flexibility in managing their security infrastructure.

  20. IP Based Fire Alarm System

    2022

    The first IP-based Fire Alarm System marks a major leap forward in fire safety and emergency management technology. By utilizing Internet Protocol (IP) connectivity, this system enables real-time monitoring and communication, allowing for faster detection and response to potential fire hazards. Users can access alerts and system status remotely, ensuring timely action even from afar. Additionally, the integration of advanced sensors enhances detection accuracy, significantly reducing false alarms and improving overall safety in residential and commercial settings.

  21. Launch of Health Monitoring System

    2023

    The first remote diagnostics system for electronic systems, known as DI-HMS , revolutionizes the way security and fire monitoring devices are managed. This innovative solution is compatible with various on-site security and fire gadgets, eliminating the need for significant capital expenditures (CapEx). DI-HMS allows for real-time monitoring and diagnostics, enhancing operational efficiency and minimizing downtime. With a strong focus on data security, it meets high-grade compliance norms, ensuring that sensitive information is protected.
